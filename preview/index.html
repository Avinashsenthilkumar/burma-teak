Introduction

More than a tree. A legacy.

Burma Teak Plant is the teak venture of Ganesh Nursery at Gandarvakottai, near Thanjavur — a nursery that has been putting saplings into Tamil Nadu soil since 1980.

Teak is the one thing a nursery sells that does not answer you quickly. A fruit tree fruits; a hedge fills in. A teak plantation replies in decades — and by then every early decision has either compounded in your favour or quietly cost you.

That long feedback loop is the whole discipline, and it is why we stay involved long after the sale would normally have ended.

              The Person Behind It

              Someone has to think in decades.

              “A nursery can sell you a plant in an afternoon. Teak asks you to care about what that plant becomes forty years from now, when the decision is no longer yours to make. That is the part I take seriously.”

              Ganesh Nursery has been putting plants into Tamil Nadu soil since 1980. Burma Teak Plant is what happens when that habit is pointed at a tree you will not live to cut.

              Where The Name Comes From

              Burma set the standard.

              Teak takes the name “Burma teak” from the forests of Myanmar, where slow growth, tight grain and clean form set the benchmark the timber trade still measures against.

              It is a standard, not a shortcut. The same quality is only reached elsewhere by giving the tree the same time and the same care — which is the whole argument for growing it properly here.
